# Independent Review: Frozen Round 03

## Decision

- **Overall score: 5/10.** Borderline paper, marginally below the acceptance threshold.
- **Recommendation: Weak Reject.**
- **Confidence: 4/5.** The method, reported finite-panel effects, and principal limitations are sufficiently inspectable. Confidence is lower for exhaustive novelty and population-level generalization, which this bounded review cannot certify.
- This is an independent assessment of the supplied frozen manuscript, not an author revision plan or a negotiation over a target score. Page length is not a decision criterion.

**Summary.** TRACE is a coherent, implementable allocation rule: construct connected predicted-query regions, preserve each region under two fixed changes to its surroundings, use the minimum of the two reset-model responses as a detached relative weight, and minimize region-averaged prompt entropy through episodic visual LayerNorm updates. The controlled evidence establishes meaningful allocation gains on COCO-CD and COCO Object at the reported configurations. The score is not an absolute safety mechanism, but the paper correctly says so. My reservation is not a discovered fatal mathematical or implementation error. It is that the strongest evidence remains development-exposed and configuration-dependent, while the specifically two-view minimum contributes little over cheaper single-view alternatives in the principal full-split results. Transparency makes those limitations assessable; it does not replace independent confirmation or establish the value of the second view.

## Prioritized Strengths

1. **S1: A real controlled allocation effect on two complete panels.** CD improves from 67.0904 with candidate-confidence allocation to 75.1048 with TRACE, and Object from 30.7642 to 32.7011, without changing candidate proposals, optimization settings, episode order, or readouts. These are much more informative for the proposed allocation rule than the cross-paper deltas. The spatial comparison also directly changes the explicit area factor at fixed support and geometry: +0.6852 on PC60 and +0.3273 on Object. References: M pp. 5-7, Tables 3 and 9; S p. 5, Table 4; `main.tex:152`, `main.tex:163`, `main.tex:170`; `generated/mechanism_status.tex`, `generated/cd_mechanism.tex`; canonical `cells/mechanism/{coco_cd,coco_object}/result.json` and product/spatial results.

2. **S2: The method is mathematically well specified and matches the checked execution path.** The text distinguishes query vocabulary from final categories, fixed diagnostic logits from gradient-bearing prompt logits, region means from token means, and episode normalization from per-image normalization. The minimum characterization is correct as an ordering identity, not a correctness theorem. Rescaling invariance and single-active-candidate cancellation are stated correctly. Inspection found the two-view subclass overrides the historical three-view evaluator, rather than silently retaining a deletion/role/dominance rule. References: M pp. 3-4 and 13-16; S pp. 2 and 4-5; `main.tex:60`, `main.tex:98`, `main.tex:119`; `appendix.tex:40`, `appendix.tex:147`; implementation `trace_ovss/method/two_view.py:30`, `trace/objective.py:16`, and `adapter.py:12` under the allowed worktree.

3. **S3: Comparison and uncertainty boundaries are mostly accurate.** Reported-reference rows are not presented as local matched baselines; the local reset-model deltas are separated from reference deltas. CD native methods retain different backends/settings, and this is disclosed. Replays are explicitly not independent seeds; shuffles are not confidence intervals; VOC20/VOC21 share images; negative and mixed results remain visible. I do not count the absence of checkpoint/readout matching in the literature rows as a misrepresentation of what this draft claims. References: M pp. 5-7, 10, 12, and 18; S pp. 1, 3, and 7; `main.tex:140`, `main.tex:144`, `main.tex:208`; `generated/reported_comparison.tex`; `appendix.tex:8` and final comparison-protocol section.

4. **S4: Substantial auditability without using labels during inference.** The image-only prediction loader and explicit scoring phase support the stated label boundary. The 41 packet file hashes match. Within the bounded checks, saved class counts reproduce all 130 scores checked across the three main suites, including smoke and replay entries, and all 30 standalone arm results. The checked metadata/seal hashes show no disagreement. These checks support record consistency, not independent re-execution or fresh validation. References: M pp. 12-14 and 17-19; S pp. 1-3 and 6-8; `appendix.tex:115`; frozen `runtime/tools/evolution/run_cell.py:77` and `:398`; packet manifest and suite/result records listed below.

## Prioritized Weaknesses

1. **W1, major: There is still no independent confirmation after evaluation-conditioned development.** Table 8 explicitly records full-validation-based LR choices for VOC20, Stuff, and CD, earlier full-score exposure elsewhere, and unknown origins for some settings. Reusing the old tuple for the final method does not make these panels untouched. Likewise, the 128-image follow-up reuses exposed evaluation images; locking new conditions before their scoring prevents additional within-suite selection but cannot undo historical exposure. The finite-panel allocation contrasts remain valid observations, but the estimate of transfer to a new evaluation population is uncertain. This is the main acceptance blocker, not inference-time label leakage. The caveat is mostly in the appendix rather than beside the headline six-split results. References: M pp. 5, 7, and 14-15, Table 8; S pp. 3-4, Table 3; `appendix.tex:91`, `appendix.tex:102`, `appendix.tex:112`; `main.tex:175`; standalone `frozen_plan.json`.

2. **W2, major: The second view and minimum have a weak accuracy/cost case.** On CD, min exceeds C-only by just **0.00018 pp**; on Object it exceeds T-only by **0.06896 pp** and Mean by **0.06492 pp**. On VOC21, PC60, and Stuff, C-only and/or Mean are better. The 128-image retained Object comparison is stronger against the full rule: T-only scores 28.1060 versus min's 27.4582 while taking 272.40 rather than 329.13 ms/image. On retained PC60, min is slightly worse than Confidence while costing 142.08 versus 69.24 ms/image. Probe complementarity establishes nonidentical assignments, not the benefit of paying for both probes. The draft acknowledges that min is not uniformly best; this is therefore an insufficient-value argument, not an accusation that it claims uniform optimality. References: M pp. 5-7 and 19, Tables 3-4, 9, and 13; S pp. 5-8, Tables 4-5 and 8; `main.tex:168`, `main.tex:181`, `main.tex:206`; generated comparison/cost tables; unrounded canonical results and standalone `rows.csv`.

3. **W3, major: Portability to different episode/optimization regimes is unresolved.** In the common-setting Object diagnostic, min is 25.9561 versus Confidence 26.4099, a **-0.4538 pp** contrast; PC60 is +0.3165. Regrouping changes PC60's min-minus-Confidence contrast from -0.0267 to +0.6091 on the same images. These are valuable counterexamples to configuration-insensitive benefit. However, only one additional ordering and one setting bundle were tested, the bundle changes several factors together, and there are no episode-level paired intervals. Exact deterministic replays do not address this uncertainty. References: M pp. 5-7 and 10, Table 4; S pp. 6-8; `main.tex:175`, `main.tex:179`, `main.tex:208`; `generated/standalone_comparison.tex`; standalone canonical arm results.

4. **W4, moderate-to-major: External-method competitiveness is not isolated.** The paper correctly labels literature values as operating-point context, but consequently Table 1 does not establish superiority over a matched local MLMP implementation. The current reset readout is already above the reported reset baseline, so some cross-paper gap plainly precedes adaptation. The CD local comparison still uses method-native backends and different adaptation settings/tuning histories. The Uniform/Confidence controls are good allocation controls, not substitutes for a close external method under a common evaluation and reasonable comparable budget. This limits significance rather than invalidating the comparisons as labeled. References: M pp. 5-6, Tables 1-2, and p. 18; S p. 7; `main.tex:138`, `main.tex:140`, `main.tex:144`; `generated/reported_comparison.tex`, `generated/cd_local.tex`; `appendix.tex` final section.

5. **W5, moderate: The mechanistic conclusion remains narrow.** Region normalization is tested on two splits, not as a broader factorial interaction with allocation or candidate construction. The shuffle is not matched on category/confidence/area and therefore cannot identify a special semantic notion of reliability. Risk strata are descriptive outcomes of a shared update, and C-to-W actually increases on Object and Stuff in the highlighted low-support group. The elementary max-lower-bound argument does not explain why the fixed transforms identify useful supervision; sensitivity to masking boundaries, color changes, context remaining inside a predicted region, and query extensions is untested. The limitations state most of this correctly, but the resulting new scientific claim is narrower than a general recipe for reliable adaptation. References: M pp. 3-5, 7, and 15-18; S pp. 4-7; `main.tex:193`, `main.tex:197`, `main.tex:212`; `appendix.tex:171` onward, especially control/interpretation definitions; `generated/update_risk.tex` and `generated/risk_strata.tex`.

6. **W6, minor: Improve prioritization and a visible figure defect.** Put a short development-exposure statement in the main protocol/results narrative and distinguish measured finite-panel improvement from prospective confirmation without forcing the reader to find Table 8. Figure 4's TRACE and Ground truth column headings visibly run together at M p. 9; separate them. This is a rendering/readability issue, not a page-count criticism. References: `main.tex:135`, `main.tex:188`; `figure/segmentation_examples.pdf`; M pp. 5 and 9.

## Minimum Actionable Repairs

1. **Add one genuinely independent confirmation protocol.** Freeze the current method, transforms, selection rule, and hyperparameters using disjoint development data, then evaluate an evaluation population/split not previously used in method/configuration decisions. Include Uniform, Confidence, T-only, C-only, and min with the same candidates/readout and all signs reported. Prioritize confirming the strongest CD/Object allocation effects. A newly sampled subset of the already exposed validation images is not this repair. No result is required to favor min.
2. **Resolve the two-view contribution at a fixed, declared operating budget.** Present accuracy and standalone cost together for the full rule and single-view alternatives. Establish a reproducible advantage or a clearly measured tradeoff on independent evidence; otherwise frame the contribution as candidate-preserving probe allocation and describe min as one conservative, sometimes dominated design choice, rather than using complementarity as a reason to pay for a second view. Do not pick a dataset-specific winner after inspecting the evaluation scores.
3. **Quantify the important uncertainty at the correct unit.** Recompute pooled mIoU in paired resamples of complete adaptation episodes from saved episode class counts, and test several predeclared regroupings with the rule frozen. In particular, show uncertainty for the small min-versus-single-view and region-versus-pixel contrasts. Such intervals are conditional on the observed protocol and do not repair evaluation-conditioned selection. Never resample candidate pixels as independent observations or relabel deterministic replays as independent seeds.
4. **Add a focused common-protocol external-method comparison.** At least on a general-purpose split supporting the claimed contribution, compare a close local reference such as MLMP under the same checkpoint identity, vocabulary, resizing, scoring, and documented comparable optimization/tuning opportunity. Report both reference-native and budget-controlled results when practical; keep the literature-reference table separate. This is additional evidence needed for competitiveness, not a correction of a fabricated matched-baseline claim.
5. **Make the textual and packaging repairs now.** Surface development exposure beside the main results; preserve all null/negative controls; repair Figure 4's headings; provide one concrete frozen-config prediction/seal/scoring command sequence or config entry point. Resolve the unknown parameter-origin fields if records exist, or retain them explicitly as unknown. A second backbone or native-resolution study would broaden scope, but neither is a blanket prerequisite for accepting a carefully bounded one-backbone contribution.

Repairs 1-4 require evidence or a material narrowing of the contribution; polishing alone would not change this decision. None authorizes running experiments during this review.
