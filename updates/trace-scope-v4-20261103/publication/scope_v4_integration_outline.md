## Scientific Argument

1. Context-probed support is an operational relative allocation signal, not a
   calibrated probability of correctness, independent risk estimate or causal
   update-utility measure.
2. Existing eight-protocol fixed-candidate comparisons remain descriptive under
   TRACE-selected and historically exposed configurations; keep WB development
   overlap explicit and separate published references from local controls.
3. The new spatial and factorial experiments localize conditional behavior:
   Object spatial contrasts support the declared preserved-region mechanism,
   PC60 spatial specificity is unresolved, and ranking/distribution effects
   depend on the other factor rather than offering a universal ranking benefit.
4. A simpler probe is not universally more accurate than the declared legacy
   components: retain Object's negative and PC60's positive primary contrasts.
5. Robustness/transfer and cost are bounded claims: SCLIP transfer is negative,
   severity5 is mixed, and V01 has no eligible near-time comparison despite its
   complete fixed grid. These are substantive findings, not execution faults.
6. Fixed real cases illustrate the measured inputs/supports/predictions only.
   Keep poor/uninformative cases and do not imply prevalence or causality.
