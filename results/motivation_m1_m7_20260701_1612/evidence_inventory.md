## Figure QA Notes

- Figure archetype: claim-primary diagnostic figures with supporting panels.
- Palette: low-saturation academic palette; no rainbow/jet color maps.
- Export: each figure is saved as editable SVG plus PNG.
- Axes: rate/proportion panels use zero-start y axes; no hidden truncation.
- Source traceability: each figure maps to a CSV table generated in this directory.

## Protocol Risks

- Single seed and internal validation split.
- Motivation diagnostics may use a bounded image sample when `--max-images` is set.
- Hard-rejection variants are diagnostic stress tests, not tuned methods.
- Region-level metrics are low-resolution patch-grid metrics, not final full-resolution benchmark mIoU.
